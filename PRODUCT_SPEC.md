# Product specification

## Product
MYReSolve Executive Risk & Cost Assessment

## Product ID
MR-PRD-001

## Governance status

The two-level Confidence and Evidence and Assurance model in this specification
was approved and locked by the Product Owner on 11 August 2026. It must not be
changed, blended into scoring or financial calculations, or replaced without a
new recorded Product Owner decision.

## Core users
- CEO
- COO
- CFO
- Functional leaders

## Assessment model
Each standard question uses a five-level maturity scale:

1. Critical
2. Developing
3. Established
4. Strong
5. Leading

After completing the assessment, the respondent gives one overall self-rated
confidence rating for how well the answers reflect how the company operates in
practice:

- Low
- Medium
- High

This overall rating leads into a separate department-level Evidence and
Assurance review. Each department is recorded as:

- Not reviewed
- Partly evidenced
- Evidence reviewed

Overall confidence and department assurance must remain visibly separate.
Neither measure changes the Health Score, Risk Score or Estimated Annual Value
at Risk. High-risk departments with low overall confidence or limited evidence
should be prioritised for review.

## Core outputs
- Executive maturity score
- Overall risk score
- Department risk scores
- Estimated annual cost of failure
- Top risks
- Recommended 90-day priorities
- Overall self-rated confidence
- Evidence and assurance by department
